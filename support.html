Support

Dura2Pleura

Didn't get the confirmation email?

Check your spam or junk folder. Links expire, so if one fails, sign up or request a reset again in the app and use the newest email.

Forgot your password?

On the app's Sign in screen, tap Forgot password?. The email link opens a page where you can choose a new one.

Something wrong with a question?

Use Report a problem under the explanation in the app. Please don't include any patient information.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account, progress and feedback.

Contact

Beta testers: contact the person who invited you. A support email address will be added before public release.
